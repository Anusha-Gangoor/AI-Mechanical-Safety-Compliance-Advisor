SECTION 1: GENERAL MACHINE SAFETY GUIDELINES

1.1 SAFETY GUARD REQUIREMENTS
- All rotating parts, pinch points, and hazardous mechanical components must be guarded.
- Safety guards must be in place and properly secured before machine operation begins.
- A machine with an open or removed safety guard must be treated as an unsafe condition.
- Safety guard interlocks must be verified functional before each shift startup.
- If a safety guard is found open or missing during operation, the machine must be stopped immediately.
- No personnel should reach into any machine zone without locking out/tagging out the energy source.

1.2 EMERGENCY STOP (E-STOP) REQUIREMENTS
- Every industrial machine must be equipped with an accessible emergency stop device.
- The emergency stop function must be operational at all times during machine operation.
- An emergency stop that is non-functional or bypassed must be considered a critical safety violation.
- Emergency stop devices must be tested at the start of each shift.
- If the emergency stop is reported as non-functional, machine operation must be suspended immediately.

1.3 TEMPERATURE OPERATING LIMITS
- Machines must operate within their manufacturer-specified temperature range.
- Normal operating temperature range for general industrial machines: 20°C to 80°C.
- Temperatures exceeding 80°C should trigger a caution alert and an operator review.
- Temperatures exceeding 100°C represent a HIGH risk condition and require immediate shutdown.
- Temperatures below 0°C may indicate abnormal operating conditions and should be investigated.
- Thermal sensors should be calibrated on a regular maintenance schedule.

1.4 PRESSURE OPERATING LIMITS
- Pressure systems must not exceed the rated maximum pressure for the equipment.
- Normal operating pressure range for general industrial machines: 0 PSI to 150 PSI.
- Pressures between 150 PSI and 200 PSI represent an elevated pressure caution zone.
- Pressures exceeding 200 PSI represent a HIGH risk condition.
- Pressure relief valves must be installed and tested regularly.
- Pressure gauges must be visible and in good calibration.

1.5 RPM (ROTATIONAL SPEED) LIMITS
- Rotating equipment must not exceed the rated maximum RPM specified by the manufacturer.
- For general industrial rotating machinery, RPM values above 3500 RPM require operator review.
- RPM values exceeding 4500 RPM represent a HIGH risk condition.
- Sudden unexplained changes in RPM should be treated as a potential fault condition.
- Overspeed protection devices must be installed and verified functional.

SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL SAFETY CONDITIONS

2.1 NORMAL OPERATION CONDITIONS
- Machine is operating within temperature, pressure, and RPM limits.
- Safety guards are in place and secured.
- Emergency stop is functional.
- No abnormal vibrations or sounds are detected.
- All safety indicators show green/normal status.

2.2 CAUTION CONDITIONS (MEDIUM RISK)
- Any single parameter approaching its upper safe operating limit.
- Safety guard found partially open or loosened but not fully removed.
- Minor temperature or pressure variations outside normal range.
- Operator-reported unusual noise or vibration.

2.3 HIGH RISK CONDITIONS
- Safety guard open, missing, or defeated.
- Emergency stop non-functional or bypassed.
- Temperature exceeding 100°C.
- Pressure exceeding 200 PSI.
- RPM exceeding 4500.
- Multiple parameters simultaneously out of range.

SECTION 3: COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

3.1 PRE-OPERATION CHECKS
- Before starting any machine, the operator must confirm:
  a) Safety guards are in place
  b) Emergency stop is functional
  c) No personnel are in the hazard zone
  d) Machine is in a known good state from prior inspection

3.2 DURING OPERATION
- Continuous monitoring of temperature, pressure, and RPM is recommended.
- Any parameter exceeding safe limits must be addressed immediately.
- Operators must not disable, remove, or bypass safety devices during operation.

3.3 DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- All safety checks must be recorded.
- Any detected non-compliance must be documented and corrected before resuming operation.
- Maintenance and safety inspection logs must be maintained.

SECTION 4: MACHINE STATES

4.1 COMPLIANT STATE
- All parameters within safe operating ranges.
- All safety devices functional.
- No outstanding safety violations.

4.2 NON-COMPLIANT STATE
- One or more parameters outside safe operating limits.
- Safety guard open or missing.
- Emergency stop non-functional.
- Unresolved safety violation from prior inspection.

4.3 INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE STATE
- When critical parameter data is missing or unavailable.
- When safety device status cannot be confirmed.
- Analysis cannot be completed without additional data.
