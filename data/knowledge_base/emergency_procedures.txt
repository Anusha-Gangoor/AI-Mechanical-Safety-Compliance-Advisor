SECTION 1: EMERGENCY RESPONSE CLASSIFICATION

1.1 EMERGENCY LEVELS
LEVEL 1 - CAUTION:
  - Single parameter approaching but not exceeding limit.
  - Safety guard partially loose (not open).
  - Unusual noise without clear cause.
  - Action: Increase monitoring frequency, review parameters, notify supervisor.

LEVEL 2 - WARNING:
  - Single parameter exceeding normal range but below high-risk threshold.
  - Safety guard found loosened or slightly open.
  - Emergency stop tested but response time slow.
  - Action: Stop non-critical operations, investigate cause, prepare for shutdown.

LEVEL 3 - CRITICAL / HIGH RISK:
  - Safety guard open or missing.
  - Emergency stop non-functional.
  - Temperature above 100°C.
  - Pressure above 200 PSI.
  - RPM above 4500.
  - Action: Immediate machine stop, evacuate hazard zone, notify safety officer.

LEVEL 4 - EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN:
  - Multiple simultaneous high-risk conditions.
  - Fire, smoke, fluid leak, structural damage.
  - Personnel injury.
  - Action: Activate emergency stop, evacuate, alert emergency services, do not re-enter area.

SECTION 2: SPECIFIC EMERGENCY RESPONSES

2.1 RESPONSE TO OPEN SAFETY GUARD
Immediate Actions:
1. Stop machine operation immediately.
2. Do not allow any personnel near the machine.
3. Identify reason for open safety guard.
4. Do not restart machine until safety guard is closed and verified.
5. Inspect safety guard for damage.
6. Document the finding.
7. Perform safety inspection before restarting.

2.2 RESPONSE TO NON-FUNCTIONAL EMERGENCY STOP
Immediate Actions:
1. Treat machine as unsafe — stop operation using other available controls.
2. Isolate power to the machine (lockout/tagout).
3. Tag machine as OUT OF SERVICE.
4. Notify maintenance personnel immediately.
5. Emergency stop must be repaired and tested before machine returns to service.
6. Do not operate machine with non-functional emergency stop under any circumstances.

2.3 RESPONSE TO HIGH TEMPERATURE
Immediate Actions:
1. Reduce machine load.
2. Check cooling system operation.
3. If temperature continues to rise toward 100°C: prepare for shutdown.
4. If temperature exceeds 100°C: initiate controlled shutdown immediately.
5. Investigate root cause before restarting.
6. Check lubrication, cooling fans, and heat exchangers.
7. Document event and corrective action.

2.4 RESPONSE TO HIGH PRESSURE
Immediate Actions:
1. Check and open pressure relief valve if accessible.
2. Reduce process input (flow, load).
3. If pressure exceeds 200 PSI: initiate immediate shutdown.
4. Do not attempt repairs on a pressurized system.
5. Depressurize safely before any maintenance.
6. Inspect pressure relief systems.
7. Document event and corrective action.

2.5 RESPONSE TO HIGH RPM / OVERSPEED
Immediate Actions:
1. Reduce machine load.
2. Check speed control system.
3. If RPM cannot be controlled: initiate emergency stop.
4. Overspeed above 4500 RPM: activate emergency stop immediately.
5. Do not attempt to physically stop rotating equipment.
6. Allow machine to come to complete rest before inspecting.
7. Check overspeed protection device.

SECTION 3: CORRECTIVE RECOMMENDATIONS FRAMEWORK

3.1 FOR SAFETY GUARD ISSUES
- Verify safety guard is properly installed and latched.
- Inspect for physical damage to the guard.
- Test safety interlock to confirm functionality.
- Do not restart machine until guard is confirmed closed and functional.
- Schedule maintenance inspection of guard hardware.

3.2 FOR EMERGENCY STOP ISSUES
- Isolate machine power immediately.
- Have qualified technician inspect and repair emergency stop circuit.
- Test emergency stop multiple times after repair.
- Confirm emergency stop performance meets requirements before returning to service.

3.3 FOR TEMPERATURE ISSUES
- Inspect cooling system (fans, heat exchangers, coolant levels).
- Reduce operational load.
- Verify temperature sensors are functioning correctly.
- Allow machine to cool before restarting.
- Investigate root cause of thermal event.

3.4 FOR PRESSURE ISSUES
- Verify pressure relief valve operation.
- Reduce inlet pressure or flow rate.
- Inspect for blockages or failed pressure regulation components.
- Depressurize safely.
- Recalibrate pressure gauges after resolution.

3.5 FOR RPM / OVERSPEED ISSUES
- Inspect and verify speed control system.
- Check overspeed protection device.
- Reduce machine load.
- Inspect drive components (belts, gears, motor controller).
- Verify tachometer/RPM sensor accuracy.
