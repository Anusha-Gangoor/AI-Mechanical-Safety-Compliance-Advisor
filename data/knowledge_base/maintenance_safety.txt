SECTION 1: LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURES

1.1 PURPOSE OF LOCKOUT/TAGOUT
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) ensures that hazardous energy is isolated and machines are properly
  de-energized before maintenance or service work is performed.
- LOTO must be applied whenever a machine guard is opened or removed for maintenance.
- Failure to apply LOTO during maintenance is a critical safety violation.

1.2 LOTO PROCEDURE STEPS
Step 1: Notify all affected employees that LOTO will be applied.
Step 2: Identify all energy sources (electrical, mechanical, pneumatic, hydraulic, thermal).
Step 3: Shut down the machine using normal stopping procedures.
Step 4: Isolate all energy sources.
Step 5: Apply lockout/tagout devices to all energy isolation points.
Step 6: Release or restrain stored energy (capacitors, springs, pressure, gravity).
Step 7: Verify energy isolation by attempting to start the machine (should not start).
Step 8: Perform the maintenance work.
Step 9: Remove all tools and personnel from the machine.
Step 10: Remove LOTO devices.
Step 11: Notify affected employees that machine is returning to service.
Step 12: Start machine and verify safe operation.

1.3 LOTO COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
- Only the employee who applied a lockout device may remove it.
- Each employee working on the machine must apply their own lock.
- LOTO devices must be uniquely identified to each employee.
- LOTO is required whenever a safety guard is removed or machine access is needed during maintenance.

SECTION 2: SAFETY INSPECTION CHECKLIST

2.1 PRE-OPERATION SAFETY INSPECTION
The following must be checked before every machine startup:
□ Safety guards: in place, secured, undamaged
□ Emergency stop: functional (press and verify)
□ Operating area: clear of personnel and obstructions
□ Control panels: no warning indicators active
□ Visible leaks: none detected
□ Unusual sounds or odors: none present
□ Previous shift log: reviewed for outstanding issues

2.2 SHIFT-END INSPECTION
□ All operating parameters returned to normal/idle range
□ No unresolved safety alerts
□ Machine left in safe state
□ Shift log completed
□ Any incidents documented

2.3 PERIODIC SAFETY INSPECTION (MONTHLY)
□ Safety guard hardware: inspect and tighten
□ Emergency stop circuitry: test and verify response time
□ Pressure relief valves: test
□ Temperature sensors: calibrate
□ Pressure gauges: calibrate
□ Speed sensors (RPM): calibrate
□ Safety interlock systems: full functional test
□ Lubrication systems: inspect
□ Wiring and connectors: visual inspection for damage

SECTION 3: MAINTENANCE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

3.1 MAINTENANCE PERSONNEL REQUIREMENTS
- Maintenance work on machines must be performed only by qualified and authorized personnel.
- Maintenance personnel must be trained in LOTO procedures.
- Maintenance personnel must not perform work outside their area of authorization.
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) must be worn during maintenance activities.

3.2 RETURN TO SERVICE AFTER MAINTENANCE
- A return-to-service inspection must be completed after any maintenance that involved:
  a) Removal of a safety guard
  b) Work on the emergency stop system
  c) Work on the drive, motor, or speed control system
  d) Work on pressure systems
  e) Work on temperature control or cooling systems

3.3 DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS FOR MAINTENANCE
- All maintenance activities must be recorded in the maintenance log.
- Maintenance log must include:
  a) Date and time of maintenance
  b) Machine ID and type
  c) Description of work performed
  d) Parts replaced or adjusted
  e) Test results after maintenance
  f) Technician name and signature
  g) Confirmation that machine was returned to safe operating state

SECTION 4: RISK-BASED MAINTENANCE PRIORITIES

4.1 HIGH PRIORITY MAINTENANCE (IMMEDIATE)
- Non-functional emergency stop
- Open or damaged safety guard
- Temperature above 100°C (unresolved)
- Pressure above 200 PSI (unresolved)
- Suspected structural damage to machine

4.2 MEDIUM PRIORITY MAINTENANCE (WITHIN SHIFT)
- Any parameter in caution range
- Safety guard hardware loose but guard not yet open
- Unusual sounds or vibrations
- Temperature in caution range (81°C - 100°C)
- Pressure in caution range (151 PSI - 200 PSI)

4.3 LOW PRIORITY MAINTENANCE (SCHEDULED)
- Routine calibration
- Preventive lubrication
- Minor wear on non-critical components
- Scheduled periodic inspections
