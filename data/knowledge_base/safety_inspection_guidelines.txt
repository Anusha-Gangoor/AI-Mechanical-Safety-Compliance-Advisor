SECTION 1: INSPECTION TYPES AND FREQUENCIES

1.1 OPERATOR INSPECTION (DAILY / EACH SHIFT)
Performed by: Machine Operator
Frequency: Before each shift startup
Focus areas:
- Visual inspection of safety guards
- Emergency stop function test
- Check for visible leaks, damage, or unusual conditions
- Review of any outstanding issues from previous shift

1.2 SUPERVISOR INSPECTION (WEEKLY)
Performed by: Safety Supervisor
Frequency: Weekly
Focus areas:
- All items in operator inspection
- Review of operator inspection logs
- Verification of safety device functionality
- Review of any incidents or near-misses from the week
- General housekeeping and workplace safety

1.3 FORMAL SAFETY INSPECTION (MONTHLY)
Performed by: Safety Officer or Qualified Inspector
Frequency: Monthly
Focus areas:
- Full functional test of all safety devices
- Calibration of sensors (temperature, pressure, RPM)
- Structural integrity of machine components
- Review of maintenance records
- Compliance with operating procedures
- Documentation review

SECTION 2: INSPECTION CRITERIA AND PASS/FAIL CONDITIONS

2.1 SAFETY GUARD INSPECTION
PASS criteria:
- Guard is installed and properly secured
- Guard shows no structural damage or deformation
- Guard interlock is functional (tested)
- Guard cannot be easily bypassed

FAIL criteria:
- Guard is missing, open, or improperly installed
- Guard is damaged and unable to provide protection
- Guard interlock fails functional test
- Guard has been bypassed or modified

Action on FAIL: Machine must not be operated until guard is repaired/replaced.

2.2 EMERGENCY STOP INSPECTION
PASS criteria:
- Emergency stop is accessible from operator position
- Emergency stop stops machine within required response time
- Emergency stop latches in activated position (cannot be accidentally released)
- Emergency stop is clearly marked and visible

FAIL criteria:
- Emergency stop does not stop machine
- Emergency stop does not latch when activated
- Emergency stop is physically damaged
- Emergency stop is blocked or not accessible

Action on FAIL: Machine must be taken out of service. Emergency stop must be repaired and retested.

2.3 TEMPERATURE MONITORING INSPECTION
PASS criteria:
- Temperature reading is within normal operating range (20°C to 80°C)
- Temperature sensor shows accurate readings (calibration verified)
- Temperature trend is stable or decreasing

FAIL criteria:
- Temperature reading above 100°C
- Temperature sensor shows erratic or invalid readings
- Unexplained rapid temperature increase

2.4 PRESSURE SYSTEM INSPECTION
PASS criteria:
- Pressure reading within normal operating range (0 to 150 PSI)
- Pressure relief valve functional (tested)
- Pressure gauges calibrated and reading correctly
- No evidence of pressure system leakage

FAIL criteria:
- Pressure reading above 200 PSI
- Pressure relief valve failed test
- Pressure gauges uncalibrated or reading incorrectly
- Evidence of pressure system leak

2.5 RPM / SPEED CONTROL INSPECTION
PASS criteria:
- RPM within rated operating range (below 3500 RPM for general machinery)
- Speed control system responds correctly to setpoint changes
- Overspeed protection device functional

FAIL criteria:
- RPM exceeding 4500 RPM
- Speed control system unresponsive
- Overspeed protection device non-functional

SECTION 3: INSPECTION DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

3.1 INSPECTION RECORD FIELDS
Each inspection record must include:
- Date and time of inspection
- Machine ID and machine type
- Inspector name
- Inspection type (operator / supervisor / formal)
- Results for each inspection item (PASS / FAIL / N/A)
- Corrective actions taken (if any)
- Outstanding issues (if any)
- Inspector signature

3.2 RECORD RETENTION
- Operator inspection records: retain for 1 year minimum (demo guideline)
- Supervisor inspection records: retain for 2 years minimum (demo guideline)
- Formal safety inspection records: retain for 5 years minimum (demo guideline)
- Incident and emergency records: retain for life of machine (demo guideline)
