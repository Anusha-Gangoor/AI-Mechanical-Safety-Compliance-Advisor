SECTION 1: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)

1.1 MACHINE STARTUP PROCEDURE
Step 1: Perform visual inspection of the machine.
Step 2: Verify all safety guards are properly installed and secured.
Step 3: Test emergency stop functionality before powering on.
Step 4: Confirm no personnel are in the machine hazard zone.
Step 5: Power on the machine and allow it to reach idle state.
Step 6: Gradually increase operating parameters to working levels.
Step 7: Monitor initial temperature, pressure, and RPM readings.
Step 8: Confirm all readings are within normal operating ranges before full operation.

1.2 MACHINE SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
Step 1: Gradually reduce operating parameters before stopping.
Step 2: Allow machine to reach idle state.
Step 3: Press stop controls and wait for full stop.
Step 4: Verify all moving parts have stopped.
Step 5: Apply lockout/tagout if maintenance is required.
Step 6: Record operational status in the shift log.

1.3 EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
Step 1: Activate emergency stop immediately.
Step 2: Alert all personnel in the vicinity.
Step 3: Do not re-start machine until the cause is identified and resolved.
Step 4: Notify maintenance and safety personnel.
Step 5: Document the incident.
Step 6: Safety inspection is required before machine can be restarted.

SECTION 2: PARAMETER OPERATING RANGES

2.1 TEMPERATURE PARAMETERS
- Normal range: 20°C to 80°C
- Caution range: 81°C to 100°C
- High risk: above 100°C
- Hypothermia risk (below freezing): below 0°C
- Action for high temperature: reduce load, check cooling system, prepare for shutdown if sustained.

2.2 PRESSURE PARAMETERS
- Normal range: 0 PSI to 150 PSI
- Caution range: 151 PSI to 200 PSI
- High risk: above 200 PSI
- Action for high pressure: open relief valve, reduce input, initiate controlled shutdown if pressure uncontrolled.

2.3 RPM PARAMETERS
- Normal range: 0 RPM to 3500 RPM
- Caution range: 3501 RPM to 4500 RPM
- High risk: above 4500 RPM
- Action for high RPM: reduce machine load, check speed control system, stop machine if RPM uncontrolled.

SECTION 3: SAFETY DEVICE OPERATING REQUIREMENTS

3.1 SAFETY GUARD OPERATION
- Safety guards must always be CLOSED during normal machine operation.
- Safety guard status must be CLOSED for a machine to be considered compliant.
- If a safety guard is OPEN, the machine is in a NON-COMPLIANT and HIGH RISK state.
- Maintenance personnel may open safety guards ONLY when the machine is fully stopped and locked out.
- Safety guards must never be opened or bypassed while the machine is in operation.

3.2 EMERGENCY STOP OPERATION
- Emergency stop must always be in FUNCTIONAL status.
- Emergency stop status of NON-FUNCTIONAL is a critical safety violation.
- Machines with non-functional emergency stops must not be operated.
- The emergency stop must be tested before each shift.
- If emergency stop is activated, the machine must remain stopped until cleared by authorized personnel.

3.3 OPERATING CONDITION CLASSIFICATION
NORMAL:
  - All parameters within safe ranges.
  - All safety devices operational.
  - No alerts or warnings active.

MAINTENANCE:
  - Machine is in planned maintenance mode.
  - Energy isolation (lockout/tagout) must be applied.
  - Safety inspection required before returning to service.

FAULT:
  - Machine has detected an abnormal condition.
  - Requires investigation before resuming operation.
  - Root cause must be identified and corrected.

EMERGENCY:
  - Emergency stop has been activated.
  - All operators must clear the hazard zone.
  - Safety personnel must assess the situation before any restart.

SECTION 4: MAINTENANCE REQUIREMENTS

4.1 SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE
- Temperature sensors: inspect and calibrate every 30 days.
- Pressure gauges: inspect and calibrate every 30 days.
- Safety guards: inspect for damage and secure mounting every 7 days.
- Emergency stop: function test every shift.
- Lubrication and mechanical components: per manufacturer schedule.
- Safety interlock systems: test every 30 days.

4.2 UNSCHEDULED MAINTENANCE TRIGGERS
- Temperature reading unexpectedly high or low.
- Pressure exceeding normal range.
- Unusual RPM fluctuation.
- Safety guard reported open or damaged.
- Emergency stop reported non-functional.
- Unusual noise, vibration, or smell from machine.

4.3 RETURN TO SERVICE AFTER MAINTENANCE
- A safety inspection must be completed after any maintenance.
- All safety guards must be re-installed and verified.
- Emergency stop must be tested.
- Operational parameters must be verified at startup.
- Inspection record must be completed before resuming operation.
